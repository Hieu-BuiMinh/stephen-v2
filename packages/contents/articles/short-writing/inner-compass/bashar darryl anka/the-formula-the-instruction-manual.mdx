This is where we give you the information that we refer to as:

- **The Formula**
- **The Instruction Manual**
- **The Cosmic Compass**

For it is literally a boiled down depiction, a boiled down description of how physical reality is created by your consciousness.

And knowing this consciously is like following an instruction manual that will allow you more conscious control over the direction in which you navigate.

## The Formula

### 1. Act on your highest passion

Act on your **highest passion every moment that you can**.

### 2. Act on it as fully as you can

Act on it:

- For as long as you can
- To the best of your ability
- Until you can act on it no further

At that point, look around for the next thing that you are capable of acting on that represents the **highest form of excitement or passion in your life**.

### 3. Have zero insistence on the outcome

Act on that passion with:

- **Absolutely zero assumption**
- **Zero insistence on what the outcome should look like**

Because the truth is:

You have no idea what the ideal outcome actually needs to look like.

### 4. Remain in a positive state

Remain in a positive state **no matter what manifests**, even if it is something you don't prefer.

If you remain in a positive state, you will always be able to use whatever manifests to your benefit.

Even if it is not something you prefer.

Because staying in the positive state will allow you to:

- See what has happened differently
- Be inspired by what has happened
- Use the situation in a way that takes you in a positive direction

### 5. Examine your belief systems

Examine your belief systems and let go of:

- Negative beliefs
- Fear-based beliefs
- Definitions that don't work for you

Because:

You are the creator of your reality.

## Why Is It Important to Act on Your Passion?

Many people have said this kind of phrase in your reality, but not many have explained **why it works** and **why it is important**.

People on your planet say:

- “Follow your dreams.”
- “Act on your bliss.”
- “Follow your passion.”

But why?

## The Physical Mind and the Higher Mind

It works like this.

You have:

- A **physical mind**, as a physical being
- A **non-physical higher mind**, as a spirit

These two things exist simultaneously.

In fact:

Spirit is your natural state.

This physical reality, in a sense, is just a dream that your spirit is having.


## Two Levels of Consciousness

You can think of your consciousness as being divided into:

#### Higher Mind

- Non-physical
- Higher frequency
- Has a broader perspective

#### Physical Mind

- Physical
- Slightly lower frequency
- Experiences physical reality directly

You need the higher mind to guide you.


## The Mountain Analogy

It's like having someone standing on a mountaintop guiding you through a valley.

From the mountaintop, they can see things you cannot see.

They can see the pitfalls and tell you: "Turn left." - "Turn right." - "Don't go there." - "You'll fall down a hole."

The physical mind needs to pay attention to this in order to be guided properly.


## Free Will

The physical mind has free will.

It can:

- Listen to the guidance
- Ignore the guidance

And if it ignores the guidance, it can often do so to its own detriment.


## How the Higher Mind Communicates

The higher mind communicates in **energy**.

It doesn't use words.

It may use:

- Pictures
- Images
- Energy

The physical body and physical mind interpret that energy through sensations such as:

- **Passion**
- **Excitement**
- **Creativity**
- **Love**


## What Passion Actually Means

When you act on your highest passion, you are acting on:

- Circumstances
- Situations
- Opportunities

that present themselves to you **synchronistically in life** and contain more excitement than any other option available at that moment.

When you feel this excitement, you are actually paying attention to the higher mind saying:

- “This is you.”
- “This is the next step on your path.”
- “This is the guidance that you're asking for.”
- “This is your sign.”


## Action Is the Language of Physical Reality

When you act on that excitement, you are responding to the higher mind.

You are essentially saying:

“All right, I heard you. I am willing to understand this is the guidance you are giving me.”

And the important thing is:

**Action is the language of physical reality.**

Not words.

Not thoughts.

**Actions.**


## Why Acting Creates More Opportunities

When you are willing to act on your passion, you are telling the higher mind:

“I am willing to listen to you.”

The higher mind can then send you more opportunities containing:

- Passion
- Excitement
- Creativity

for you to act on.

But if you're not willing to act on what the higher mind has already sent you:

Why should it send you more opportunities that contain excitement?

Because you're not going to act on them.


## Start the Dialogue

You have to start the ball and the dialogue rolling.

That means being willing to trust that this excitement is guidance coming from your higher mind.

It is telling you:

Exactly what the next step in your life is.


## Why This Can Feel Confusing

For many humans, this may be confusing and difficult.

Because what excites you may seem to have:

- Absolutely nothing to do with supporting you in life
- Absolutely nothing to do with what you were doing a moment ago
- No logical connection to where you think you are supposed to go

But that doesn't mean it is disconnected.


## Excitement Is the Connective Thread

The excitement itself is the connection.

It is the connective thread that lets you know it is connected.

Even if your physical mind cannot figure out **how** it is connected.

You have to trust that:

**The path of excitement is the thread to follow.**

That is the true path for you.

And it is representative of:

**The true core vibration of your being.**


## The Formula in Short

1. **Act on your highest passion.**
2. **Act on it as fully and as long as you can.**
3. **Do not insist on a particular outcome.**
4. **Remain in a positive state regardless of what happens.**
5. **Examine and release negative or fear-based beliefs.**

And remember:

**Passion and excitement are the signals.**

**Action is your response.**

**Excitement is the thread that shows you the path.**
