### Adam and Eve

The discussion begins with the names:

- **Adam and Eve**
- **Adama and Eva**

These are described as:

- The **male line**
- The **female line**

of the first humans allegedly created by the **Anunnaki**.

---

## The Question

The question being asked is:

What is the actual story behind the Garden of Eden, the Tree of Knowledge, the snake, the apple, and everything associated with them?

---

## The Anunnaki and Human Origins

According to the explanation, an extraterrestrial race known as:

- **The Anu**
- **The Anunnaki**

came to Earth and combined their genetic material with naturally evolved hominids.

These hominids are described as being broadly related to:

- **Homo erectus**
- **Homo habilis**

This genetic manipulation is said to have occurred approximately:

**300,000–500,000 years ago**

The result of this genetic fusion is described as:

**Homo sapiens**

---

## The Snake

The snake is presented as a **symbol of knowledge and wisdom**.

More specifically, it allegedly represents the Anunnaki's knowledge of:

**Genetics**

The connection is made through the spiral shape of the snake.

#### Snake → Spiral → DNA

The explanation connects this symbolism with the modern medical **caduceus**, which often depicts two intertwined snakes.

According to Bashar:

The double snakes represent the idea of DNA.

---

## The Tree of Knowledge

The Anunnaki allegedly did not want humans to possess the same knowledge that they possessed.

They wanted humans to remain:

**Somewhat subservient.**

Therefore, gaining or “eating” knowledge became something that was considered:

**Forbidden**

So the Biblical idea of eating from the **Tree of Knowledge** is interpreted symbolically as humans gaining access to knowledge that their creators supposedly did not want them to possess.

---

## The Garden of Eden

According to the explanation, the Garden of Eden was not necessarily one literal garden.

Instead, there were supposedly multiple locations around Earth where the Anunnaki operated:

**Genetic laboratories**

One of the primary locations was allegedly in what we now call:

**The Middle East**

More specifically:

Near the **Euphrates River**

This location became associated with the original **Garden of Eden**.

---

## The Meaning of “Eden”

Bashar claims that:

**Eden was simply the name given to that particular base or area.**

The pronunciation is emphasized as:

**E-Den**

or

**Eden**

In this interpretation, Eden was therefore not originally a supernatural paradise.

It was supposedly:

**A particular Anunnaki installation or region.**

---

## The Anunnaki Broke Their Own Laws

The explanation then suggests that the majority of the Anunnaki did **not** come to Earth.

Eventually, those Anunnaki supposedly discovered what the group on Earth had done.

The genetic creation of humans was allegedly:

**Against their laws.**

As a result:

The Anunnaki who came to Earth were recalled.

---

## Humanity Had Already Been Created

There was now a problem.

Humans had already been created.

Therefore, humanity could not simply be abandoned entirely.

The Anunnaki supposedly understood that humans would need:

**Guidance**

So from time to time, some of them allegedly returned to help guide early humanity.

---

## Dormant Genetic Markers

Even after humanity had been created, humans supposedly still had many:

**Genetic markers turned off**

This meant that humans did not yet have access to their full potential or understanding.

Some Anunnaki then became:

- Guides
- Teachers
- Tutors

They supposedly helped humanity develop over time.

---

## Eventually Humanity Had to Grow Alone

At a certain point, however, humanity had to be allowed to evolve independently.

Humans had to discover:

- What they would do
- How they would use information
- How they would develop consciousness
- How they would interpret teachings

They received information from various alleged:

**Off-world sources**

But eventually had to make their own choices.

---

## Why Humans Saw the Anunnaki as Gods

Because of the way early humanity was supposedly created and guided, early humans interpreted the Anunnaki as:

**Gods**

Bashar argues that early humans encountered beings who appeared:

- More advanced
- More knowledgeable
- More technologically capable
- Responsible for their creation

Therefore, humans naturally interpreted them as divine beings.

---

## The Origin of Religion

According to Bashar, this creator–creation relationship became the foundation for:

**Religion**

The argument is that religion developed because humans believed there were superior beings:

- Above humanity
- Greater than humanity
- Responsible for humanity
- Capable of judging or controlling humanity

Without this relationship, Bashar argues:

Humanity would not have religion in the form it exists today.

---

## Humans Would See Themselves as God

Without the idea of superior external beings, humans would supposedly recognize:

**Ourselves as God**

This does not necessarily mean being an individual omnipotent deity.

Rather, it means seeing ourselves as:

**Direct aspects of Creation**

Human beings would have a direct spiritual connection with existence itself.

---

## No Need for Intermediaries

If humans understood this direct relationship with Creation, according to Bashar, there would be no need for:

- Priests
- Religious authorities
- Spiritual intermediaries
- Superior beings standing between humans and God

You would not believe that someone else was:

- Better than you
- Greater than you
- Above you
- Spiritually closer to Creation than you

Instead:

You would have a direct spiritual connection to Creation.

---

## Commandments, Laws, and Religious Rules

Bashar then discusses:

- Commandments
- Laws
- Religious rules

These are described as:

**Very limited interpretations of a deeper underlying knowledge.**

The original teachings allegedly contained a greater understanding.

But humans interpreted them through their own limited capacity.

---

## Why the Knowledge Was Misinterpreted

The explanation claims that early humans did not yet possess the genetic capacity to fully understand the information they were being given.

Therefore:

The original knowledge became misinterpreted.

Over time, deeper ideas supposedly became simplified into:

- Rules
- Laws
- Commandments
- Religious doctrines

---

## Humanity Needed Time

The guides allegedly understood that human development would take a long time.

According to Bashar, this process has taken:

**Many thousands of years**

Humanity has gradually developed to the point where it can begin understanding these deeper ideas.

---

## “Turning On” Genetic Markers

Bashar describes modern human development as:

**Opening up genetic markers that were previously turned off**

This process is linked to:

- Remembering who we are
- Expanding consciousness
- Becoming more spiritually aware

Eventually, humanity becomes capable of recognizing:

**We are aspects of All That Is.**

---

## “All That Is”

The central spiritual idea is that every person is an aspect of:

**All That Is**

Rather than being fundamentally separate from Creation, humans are portrayed as expressions of the same larger existence.

Therefore:

Divinity is not something completely outside of you.

Instead:

You are an aspect of it.

---

## Why This Information Can Be Discussed Now

Bashar argues that humanity has reached a level of consciousness where conversations like this can now occur.

Humanity's:

**Vibrational state**

has supposedly become sufficiently compatible with this information.

This allows beings like Bashar and others to share it.

---

## Information Must Be Useful

According to Bashar, information should only be introduced when humanity is capable of using it constructively.

Otherwise, it could simply:

**Confuse people**

The idea is that humanity has now reached a point where these concepts can potentially:

**Serve human development rather than merely create confusion.**

---

## But Misinterpretation Still Happens

Even now, Bashar acknowledges that:

There is still a great deal of misinterpretation.

Information given by these alleged sources can still be:

- Misunderstood
- Distorted
- Taken literally
- Turned into beliefs or doctrines

So the process of understanding continues.

---

## The Story in Short

According to the narrative presented in the video:

1. **The Anunnaki came to Earth.**
2. They genetically combined their DNA with existing hominids.
3. This allegedly contributed to the creation of **Homo sapiens**.
4. **Adam and Eve** symbolize early male and female human lines.
5. The **snake** represents knowledge, genetics, and DNA.
6. The **Tree of Knowledge** represents forbidden knowledge.
7. **Eden** was allegedly an Anunnaki genetic laboratory or base near the Euphrates.
8. The genetic experiment supposedly violated Anunnaki laws.
9. The Anunnaki involved were recalled.
10. Some later returned as teachers or guides.
11. Early humans interpreted these advanced beings as **gods**.
12. This relationship allegedly contributed to the development of **religion**.
13. Religious commandments became simplified interpretations of deeper knowledge.
14. Humanity has gradually expanded its consciousness over thousands of years.
15. Humans are now beginning to recognize themselves as:

**Aspects of All That Is.**

---

## Core Idea

The deeper philosophical message of the video is:

**Human beings should not see themselves as spiritually inferior beings separated from God or Creation.**

Instead:

**Every person is directly connected to Creation and is an expression of it.**

The Anunnaki story is used as an explanation for why humanity supposedly lost that understanding and began placing divinity outside itself.
