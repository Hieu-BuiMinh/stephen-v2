Amidst the fragrant aroma of warm tea and the quiet stillness of the room, the noise of the outside world simply faded away, leaving behind a profound sense of inner peace.

The profound experience of this gathering unfolded over two serene days: starting on a tranquil Thursday afternoon, May 14th, and carrying its gentle resonance into the bright morning of Saturday, May 16th.

## The Anticipation

Arriving early at the venue, I took a moment to wander and admire the meticulously arranged space before entering the main tea room. The environment itself felt like a gentle preparation for the mind. This beautiful sanctuary was [Kosala](https://www.facebook.com/KosalaVN), a dedicated Vietnamese Teahouse ([Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/W964jwobCRc8yDBa8)), where their deep passion for organic, premium tea culture is felt in every detail.

The shelves were gracefully adorned with an exquisite collection of traditional teapots. Each piece seemed to carry its own unique history and character, quietly resting as if waiting to tell a story.

## The Master's Arrival

When Master Giac Nguyen finally entered the room, the atmosphere subtly shifted. There was a profound sense of reverence that swept through the space, yet his presence was incredibly warm, welcoming, and grounding.

We were treated to delicate pastries that the Master had thoughtfully prepared for us beforehand. During the session, we had the distinct privilege of savoring two exceptional varieties of tea: the rare Dragon Claw (Móng Rồng) and the raw ancient Shan Tuyet harvested from the majestic Tay Con Linh mountains in Ha Giang. The exquisite taste of these precious teas seemed to resonate perfectly with the profound Dharma talk being shared. I felt an overwhelming sense of gratitude, realizing what an incredibly auspicious and rare destiny (_nhân duyên_) it was to be there—to be enlightened by the Master's teachings while partaking in such magnificent tea.

## Words of Wisdom

As we sat together, sipping our tea, we listened attentively to his precious teachings. His words were simple, yet they carried a profound depth, touching upon the art of living consciously and finding inner stillness amidst the chaos of modern life.

The resonance of Thursday's session carried over beautifully into Saturday morning. After another enlightening Dharma talk, we gathered together to immortalize the serene moments shared with the Master and fellow practitioners.
